How it works

This solver uses Herbert Kociemba's two-phase algorithm. Solutions are short but not always the shortest possible; they stay within 22 moves. The cube state is represented as a 54-character facelet string, nine stickers per face, ordered U, R, F, D, L, B.

Type moves directly (U, U', D, D', L, L', R, R', F, F', B, B') or use the controls above. The 3D cube responds to mouse drag for camera rotation.

Keyboard shortcuts: Press S to scramble, Enter to solve, arrow keys to step through the solution, and letter keys (U, D, L, R, F, B) to turn layers. Hold Shift for prime (counter-clockwise) moves.
